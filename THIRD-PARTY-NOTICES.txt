SAROO — tpunix
https://github.com/tpunix/SAROO

RAW-TURN extends the SAROO firmware for the STM32 and the Saturn; the FPGA
stays unchanged. The SAROO sources carry no license file. For that reason
neither this repository nor the program contains SAROO source files or
SAROO firmware. The program carries patches only, which it applies on your
PC to the official SAROO release v0.9, taken from your card or downloaded
from the SAROO release page. The folder saroo holds only my own additions to
the firmware, for reading.

saroo/saturn/ldscript_kartusche builds on the Saturn linker script by
Anders Montonen, based on Bart Trzynadlowski's Custom Sega Saturn Linker
Script, both public domain.
